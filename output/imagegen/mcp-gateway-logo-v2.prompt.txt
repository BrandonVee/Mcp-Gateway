Use case: logo-brand.
Asset: one finished app icon for MCP Gateway, an open-source infrastructure platform that gives MCP clients a unified authenticated entry point and routes requests to many upstream MCP servers.
Design an original, highly legible geometric gateway emblem. The core idea is ONE entry connecting multiple upstream services: two short blue and cyan route segments converge through a single strong open portal. The overall negative space subtly suggests a capital G for Gateway. Resolve the routes and portal into one compact, coherent mark with two or three solid shapes, thick consistent widths, generous clear gaps and softly rounded engineered corners. It should feel like professional developer infrastructure, precise and trustworthy, and remain instantly recognizable at 24 or 32 pixels. Make the gateway/connection idea more apparent than a decorative letter.
Palette: electric blue #2F8CFF and cyan #22D3EE on a completely opaque, uniform dark navy #070B13 canvas. The navy background fills the entire square, with no frame or enclosing outline. Solid fills only, clean smooth vector-like contours.
Composition: square canvas, exactly one centered symbol, large and optically balanced; mark occupies about 80 percent of the canvas with equal padding. Produce the icon itself, not a mockup or branding board.
Avoid: tiny branches, thin strokes, loose ribbons, a cloud, a four-square dashboard grid, shield, padlock, robot, sparkle, generic hub-and-spoke nodes, isometric cube, circuit-board ornament, wordmark, text, captions, gradients, glow, shadows, photographic texture, 3D, speckles, rough edges, watermark or multiple variants.
